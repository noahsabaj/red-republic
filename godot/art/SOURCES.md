# Where the art came from

Written by `tools/fetch_art.py`. Everything below is **CC0** from
[ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) — public domain, no attribution required.
It is recorded anyway: CC0 does not oblige anyone to say where a file came
from, and being unable to answer that about your own repository is its own
problem.

Downloaded at 1K, keeping colour, OpenGL-convention normal and
roughness. Displacement and ambient occlusion are in the source archives and
are deliberately not kept — this terrain is a heightmapped mesh with its own
relief, and a second displacement fighting it is worse than none.

| used as | ambientCG asset | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `grass` | [Grass004](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Grass004) | Meadow grass, mixed green with dry blades and visible soil. |
| `forest` | [Ground003](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground003) | Forest floor: needles, leaf litter and twigs. |
| `rock` | [Rock030](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rock030) | Bare grey rock, for slopes too steep to hold soil. |
| `dirt` | [Ground037](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground037) | Bare earth, for worn ground and cuttings. |
| `shore` | [Gravel022](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Gravel022) | Water-worn gravel, for the margin where land meets water. |
| `snow` | [Snow006](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Snow006) | Fresh snow with a wind-textured surface. |
